About

I am a Research Scientist at the Canadian Centre for Climate Modelling and Analysis (CCCma), Environment and Climate Change Canada, based in Victoria, British Columbia.

I examine global climate change and variability using Earth System Models and observations. My particular areas of focus have been model development, evaluation and uncertainty, the Southern Ocean in the climate system, the role of the ocean in the global carbon cycle, sea-ice change and variability in both the Arctic and Antarctic, and climate stabilization.

I coordinate development of the Canadian Earth System Model (CanESM), and its application in the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project, providing experience in managing large teams and projects.

Research Focus

Advancing our understanding of the climate system through modelling and analysis

Southern Ocean & Sea Ice

Investigating the physical drivers of Southern Ocean warming, sea ice variability, and water mass transformation — including the role of wind forcing, freshwater fluxes, and stratification feedbacks in shaping the polar climate system.

Climate Modelling (CanESM)

Leading the development of the Canadian Earth System Model (CanESM), Canada's primary tool for climate projection and CMIP participation. Responsible for model development, evaluation, and large-ensemble experiments used worldwide.

Ocean–Atmosphere Interactions

Examining how ocean circulation, heat uptake, and carbon storage respond to changing atmospheric forcing, with a focus on attribution, detection, and the interplay between natural variability and anthropogenic trends.

Carbon Cycle & Earth System Feedbacks

Studying the role of the ocean and terrestrial biosphere in the global carbon cycle, including methane, permafrost, and vegetation feedbacks, to constrain projections of future atmospheric CO₂ and global temperature.

Detection & Attribution

Developing and applying statistical frameworks to detect anthropogenic climate signals and attribute observed changes to specific forcing agents, informing both scientific understanding and policy-relevant climate assessments.

Open Science & Research Software

Committed to open, reproducible climate science. Developing Python-based analysis tools and workflows and contributing to community software and data standards that underpin modern climate research.

Major Projects

Leading and contributing to national and international climate research initiatives

Climate Modelling

CanESM — Canadian Earth System Model

I coordinate development of the Canadian Earth System Model (CanESM), and its application in the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project (CMIP). As team lead of the CanESM development team I manage a team of scientists and technical staff, including work planning, career development and administrative management.

CCCma GitLab
International Initiative

SOFIA — Southern Ocean Freshwater Input from Antarctica

The Southern Ocean Freshwater Input from Antarctica (SOFIA) Initiative is an international model intercomparison project concerned with the scientific objectives and experimental design needed to understand the effects of Antarctic meltwater on the Southern Ocean and global climate system. See: Swart et al. (2023), Geosci. Model Dev., 16, 7289–7309.

National Initiative

CP4C — Climate Prediction for Canada

Climate Prediction for Canada (CP4C) is a national initiative to develop and deliver climate predictions on seasonal to decadal timescales for Canada, underpinned by CanESM model development and large ensemble simulations.

Model Evaluation

CanTODS — Canadian Trend and Observational Diagnostics Suite

CanTODS is a software suite for the systematic evaluation of climate model trends against observations, developed to support transparent and reproducible assessment of CanESM and other CMIP models.

GitHub
Large Ensemble

CanESM Large Ensemble

Large initial-condition ensembles of CanESM2 and CanESM5 simulations used to separate the forced climate change signal from internal variability, and widely applied in detection and attribution research and climate projection studies.
