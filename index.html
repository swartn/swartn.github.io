Research Themes

Using Earth System Models and observations to advance understanding of the climate system and to deliver useful climate information to stakeholders

Polar oceans & Sea Ice

  • Drivers of Southern Ocean warming & freshening
  • Arctic & Antarctic sea-ice variability and change
  • Trends in the Southern Hemisphere westerlies & their impact on ocean circulation and climate
  • Impact of Antarctic meltwater on oceans and climate
    • Swart & Fyfe 2013 · Swart et al. 2015 · Swart et al. 2015b · Swart et al. 2018 · Liu et al. 2022 · Chen et al. 2023 ·

    Carbon Cycle and Climate

  • The role of the ocean in the global carbon cycle — quantifying ocean carbon uptake and its uncertainty using models and observations.
  • How wind-driven circulation and model biases affect ocean carbon uptake and storage, particular in the Southern Ocean.
  • Marine Carbon Dioxide Removal
  • Climate/emissions targets and stabilization
    • Swart & Fyfe 2012 · Swart et al. 2014 · Gooya et al. 2024 · Duke et al. 2024 · Duke et al. 2023

    Global Earth System Model & Infrastructure Development

  • Development, evaluation and application of coupled Earth System Models - notably CanESM.
  • Model coupling, tuning and optimization on high performance computers
  • Development of open software frameworks to enable efficient, reproducible and collaborative climate modelling - notably the Integrated Modelling System Infrastructure (IMSI).
    • IMSI repository · CanESM repository

    Canadian focused predictions and projections

    Developing high resolution systems to downscale global climate models to high resolution over Canada. Development of the Canadian Three Oceans Downscaling System (CanTODS) for consistent downscaling of ocean climate. Coupling of the Canadian Regional Climate Model (CanRCM) and CanTODS to form the Canadian Regional Earth System Model to resolve coupled feedbacks at high resolution over Canada.

    Major Projects

    Named initiatives, models, and tools I lead or co-lead

    National Earth System Model

    CanESM — Canadian Earth System Model

    Within CCCma I coordinate a team of over 40 scientists in the development and application of the Canadian Earth System Model (CanESM). We are currently finalizing CanESM6 - a major advance towards a high resolution capable ESM. I'm the CCCma Science lead for the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 7 (CMIP7). I coordinated the finalization of CanESM5 and its contribution to CMIP6, and led the first open source release of CanESM and its implementation as a portable, collaborative tool.

    Swart et al. 2019 Sigmond et al. 2023 CanESM gitlab
    Open science Initiative

    CP4C — Collaborative Platform for CanESM

    I'm a founder and ECCC reprsentative on the steering committee member of the Collaborative Platform for CanESM (CP4C) - an initiative to foster collaboration in the development and application of the Canadian Earth System Model between CCCma and the broader external climate research community. CP4C enables researchers to use CanESM on external to ECCC high performance computers, and in particular on the Digitial Research Alliance of Canada platforms. CP4C has over 50 members at universities across Canada, has enabled external-to-ECCC research with CanESM for the first time, and has made novel science contributions, including to the Regional Aerosol Model Intercomparison Project (RAMIP).

    CP4C website CP4C GitLab
    International Initiative

    SOFIA — Southern Ocean Freshwater Input from Antarctica

    The Southern Ocean Freshwater Input from Antarctica (SOFIA) Initiative is an international scientific collaboration designed to understand the effects of Antarctic meltwater on the Southern Ocean and global climate system. I co-led the creation of SOFIA through the Southern Ocean Regional Panel(SORP). SOFIA is a recognized CLIVAR task team adjunct a registered CMIP7 Community MIP. MIP

    Swart et al. 2023 SOFIA website SOFIA CLIVAR task team page
    Downscaling for NAS

    CanTODS — Canadian Three Oceans Downscaling System

    The Canadian Three Oceans Downscaling System (CanTODS) is designed as a collaborative open framework to consistenly downscale cliamte across Canada's surrounding oceans. I coordinated the CanTODS 2018 workshop, bringing together researchers from ECCC, Fisheries and Oceans Canada and academia. I led the funding proposal for CanTODS under the Canadian Focused Predictions and Projections theme of Canada's National Adaptation Stategy (NAS), and I play an active role in CanTODS development and science leadership within CCCma.
